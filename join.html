Join the lab

We work at the seam between communication theory and machine learning. That seam is where most of our openings are.

Nobody arrives fluent in both halves. What we look for is someone strong in one and genuinely curious about the other: a communication student willing to learn Python, or a computer science student willing to take message effects seriously. If you can already do both, say so early.

Graduate students

M.A. in Communication

Apply to the UND Department of Communication and name KIKI Lab in your statement. Graduate research assistantships are tied to funded projects, so timing matters — ask before the application deadline rather than after.

Undergraduates

Research assistants

UND undergraduates join for course credit or through the College of Arts & Sciences Undergraduate Research fund. Expect real tasks: coding stimuli, running participants, cleaning data, building prompts.

Collaborators

Faculty and industry

We collaborate across communication, computer science, public health, psychology, and political science. If you have a dataset, an instrument, or a question that needs both halves of this lab, write.

Writing a useful first email

Four short paragraphs beat a long one. Include:

  • Which research area interests you, by name — WB, COMP, HLTH, or FRAME.
  • One paper of ours you have read, and one thing you would do differently.
  • What you can already do: statistics, Python, qualitative coding, design, recruiting.
  • Your timeline — the semester you would start, and whether you need funding.
